# Resolution Report

The **Resolution Report** is the final operational summary that closes the loop between support, development, QA, and the requester.

---

## Purpose

The report captures:

- What was done
- What was validated
- Final outcome and any follow-up actions

It becomes the historical reference for audits and future incident analysis.

---

## Who Creates It

**Primary owner:** Support Analyst  
**Inputs from:** DEV Manager, Developer, Requester/UAT representative

---

## When It Is Created

Create after:

1. QA/UAT result is available
2. Deployment state is known
3. Requester confirmation has been received (where applicable)

---

---

## Example Outcome Statement

"Issue resolved in release v1.8.4; requester confirmed successful PAYE submission and e-slip generation in production on 2026-04-13. Ticket moved to **Resolved** and then **Closed**."

---

## Quality Checklist

- Technical and business outcome both recorded
- Version/deployment details included
- Evidence references included
- Next actions clear (if any)

---

## Link Back to Process

This report should be attached or referenced in the ticket thread before final closure.
